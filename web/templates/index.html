Authoritative Regulatory & Standard References
NIST Special Publication 800-226
Guidelines for Evaluating Differential Privacy Guarantees (March 2025). Guidance on evaluating differential privacy guarantees: privacy parameters ε and δ, units of privacy, mechanisms, and deployment hazards. It gives no fixed numeric limits; the thresholds used here are DP-Advice defaults.
W3C Privacy Principles Statement
W3C Statement (May 2025) covering Data Minimization (§2.2), De-identified Data (§2.6) and Transparency (§2.11). It does not address differential privacy directly; DP-Advice draws on it for general principles only.
